The Ferris wheel had barely reached the top when the lights below suddenly went out. Mia laughed at first, thinking it was part of the carnival show, but then the wheel jerked to a stop, leaving her dangling high above the darkened fairgrounds. A cold wind rattled the metal seat as the cheerful music faded into silence. Just when Mia began to panic, she noticed a small glow in the distance—the carnival’s emergency crew rushing toward the ride. Moments later, the lights flickered back on, and the wheel slowly began to move again. When Mia finally stepped onto solid ground, she realized that what had started as an ordinary carnival ride had become the most unforgettable adventure of her summer.
