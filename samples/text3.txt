Digital safety is important because so much of our daily lives takes place online. Using strong, unique passwords, keeping personal information private, and being cautious when clicking links or downloading files can help protect people from scams, identity theft, and other online threats. It is also important to use privacy settings and think carefully before sharing photos, messages, or other information on social media. By practicing good digital safety habits, people can enjoy the benefits of technology while reducing the risks that come with being connected to the internet.
